# SUMOPED — Branch inventory of `MSPModel_Striping` (SUMO 1.20.0, tag `v1_20_0`)

**Status: PROPOSAL — generated as task SP-0.0's first pass, pending review.**

Every behavioural branch whose outcome can change a pedestrian trajectory, with a stable ID, its C++
predicate, and whether it is observable in person FCD. This is the denominator of the coverage claim in
`SUMOPED-COVERAGE.md` §1: each ID must be witnessed by a named scenario + oracle signal, or listed as an
admitted hole. **148 branch rows** in this pass (plus the HIDDEN / saturation / multi-lane summary tables).

Observability legend: `DIRECT` (shows in pos/speed/edge immediately) · `LATERAL` (only via world x/y —
note person FCD has **no** `posLat`, so lateral state must be back-derived by projecting x/y onto the
lane centreline) · `INDIRECT` (visible only through a downstream consequence) · `HIDDEN` (not in FCD at
all — see the HIDDEN section for the cheapest signal that does witness it).

> **Corrections applied to this pass** (verified against `/sumo/src/microsim/MSFrame.cpp:522,538,541`):
> - `pedestrian.striping.stripe-width` default is **0.64 m**, not 0.65 — a few rows below say 0.65.
> - `pedestrian.striping.reserve-oncoming` default is **0.0**; **0.34** is the *junctions* variant
>   (`reserve-oncoming.junctions`). Consequence for coverage: `WALK-RESERVE-ONCOMING-STRIPES` **cannot
>   fire on a normal sidewalk at default settings** — only on crossings/walkingareas. Witnessing the
>   sidewalk variant needs a scenario that sets `--pedestrian.striping.reserve-oncoming` explicitly.
> - The jam counter is exposed by `--person-summary-output` (per-step `jammed` column) and
>   `--statistic-output` (`<persons ... jammed="N"/>`), both verified this session.
>
> **Observability upgrade — `LATERAL` is stronger than it looks.** `PState::getAngle`
> (`MSPModel_Striping.cpp:2342-2349`) returns `laneRotation ± atan2(mySpeedLat, max(mySpeed, eps))`,
> so the FCD `angle` attribute **directly encodes `mySpeedLat`**. Verified empirically on a saturated
> crossing: inverting it as `|mySpeedLat| = speed * tan(angle - laneBearing)` over 1805 samples
> recovered a maximum of **0.6401 m/s** — exactly the model's `stripeWidth` clamp — with a strong
> secondary mode at **0.5556 m/s** = `vMax * LATERAL_SPEED_FACTOR` (1.3889 x 0.4). Both theoretical
> caps land on the nose, which validates the inversion.
>
> Consequence: from a single FCD row we can recover `myRelX` (`pos`), `myRelY` (project `x,y`),
> `mySpeed` (`speed`), `mySpeedLat` (`angle`), the lane (`edge`) and `myDir`. **Only `myAmJammed`,
> `myWaitingTime`, `myWaitingToEnter`, `myNLI` and `myWalkingAreaPath` stay unobserved** — and
> `myAmJammed` has the `jammed` counter + stderr warning, while `myWaitingTime` is largely derivable
> from consecutive `speed < 0.1` steps. Rows marked `LATERAL` below should therefore be read as
> "observable, via `angle` and/or projected `x/y`", not "weakly observable".

adds `atan2(mySpeedLat, speed)` to the lane-tangent heading, so a *sidestep* is actually **DIRECT**
via `angle`, in addition to being LATERAL via `x,y` — flagged explicitly below where it applies.

## Branches requiring saturation (crowding or heavy vehicle traffic)

- **XVEH-ALLBLOCKED-PINALL** — needs enough simultaneous vehicles crossing (or one very slow one) that *every* non-reserved stripe of the crossing is covered at once; roughly ≥(stripes − reserved) vehicles occupying distinct lateral bands, or a 1-2 stripe crossing with just 1-2 vehicles. Reserved band is `floor(stripes*0.5 default reserve-oncoming.junctions)`, so a 2-stripe crossing needs only 1 vehicle to trivially saturate; a 6-stripe crossing needs ~3+ simultaneous vehicles across its width.
- **WALK-JAM-TRIGGER / WALK-JAM-CROSSING-THRESHOLD / WALK-JAM-NARROW-THRESHOLD** — need sustained (≥`jamTime`/`jamTimeCrossing`/`jamTimeNarrow`, default 10 s) *complete* blockage, i.e. every stripe simultaneously blocked for the full duration — practically a queue depth of at least `stripes+1` peds converging from both directions on a corridor, or a single-stripe (`sMax==0`) corridor with 2 opposing peds that never yield. A rough density benchmark: >1 ped per stripe-metre sustained for 10 s reliably produces this in practice.
- **WALK-UNJAM** — the mirror image: needs the same crowd to *thin out* again, so the test scenario needs a transient surge (e.g. a signal-release pulse) rather than steady-state crowding.
- **NEIGH-FIRST-BLOCKER-WINS / NEIGH-OVERLAP-SELF-STRIPE-IGNORE** — need ≥3 peds queued single-file (same stripe) to distinguish "first blocker wins" from "last blocker wins"; 2 peds cannot expose the ordering rule.
- **MIDOL-SELF-AS-OBSTACLE-FOR-FOLLOWERS** in a *chain* (not just one pair) — needs ≥3 peds in a queue to confirm the obstacle propagates transitively step-by-step rather than only to the immediate follower.
- **XVEH-CLOSEST-WINS** — needs ≥2 vehicles queued to cross the same crossing lane simultaneously.
- **VEHOBS-STRIPE-RANGE-Y** at its most interesting (partial multi-stripe straddle) — needs a lane ≥3 stripes wide and a vehicle wider than one stripe (any car already is, given default `stripeWidth=0.65m`; a *clean* single-stripe-vs-multi-stripe A/B needs a deliberately narrow stripe-width or a deliberately wide (bus/truck) vType).
- **WA-VEHFOE-BETWEEN-POINTS** — needs a walkingarea wide enough (front/back corners of one vehicle footprint mapped onto the path have to be >1 `stripeWidth` apart) — effectively a vehicle crossing at a shallow angle relative to the pedestrian path, or a walkingarea several metres across.

## Branches requiring multi-lane roads / wide crossings

- **WALK-RESERVE-ONCOMING-STRIPES** — `getReserved` = `floor(stripes*RESERVE_FOR_ONCOMING_FACTOR)` with default factor 0.34 (open sidewalks) / higher on junctions (`RESERVE_FOR_ONCOMING_FACTOR_JUNCTIONS`); needs `stripes>=3` (≈2 m sidewalk at default 0.65 m stripe width) for the reserved band to be non-empty at all on open sidewalks; junctions saturate the reservation sooner.
- **NEXTOBS-STRIPEEND-WALL / MOVENEXT-STRIPE-OFFSET-ADJUST / NEXTOBS-STRIPE-OFFSET-REMAINDER** — need two adjoining lanes/walkingareas of *different* stripe counts, i.e. a sidewalk-width change at a junction (e.g. a 3 m sidewalk narrowing to a 1.5 m crossing).
- **VEHOBS-STRIPE-RANGE-Y / WA-VEHFOE-BETWEEN-POINTS** — need either a multi-lane road (wide vehicle footprint spanning several ped stripes) or a walkingarea several metres wide.
- **XVEH-ALLBLOCKED-PINALL / XVEH-FROMLEFT-MIRROR** — a multi-lane crossing (≥2 vehicle lanes) with traffic approaching the crossing from both the left and right side of the pedestrian's walking direction is needed to see the mirrored `fromLeft()` placement and a realistic "all stripes blocked" scenario (a single-lane crossing saturates trivially with 1 vehicle and doesn't exercise the mirroring).
- **NEXTLANE-WA-ROUTE-FOUND / NEXTLANE-WA-ARRIVALPOS / WA-PEDTRANSFORM-OTHERPATH-PROJECT** — need a walkingarea with **≥3** incident sidewalks (a "T" or "+" junction) — a plain 2-sidewalk walkingarea (straight-through) never has more than the one "same path"/"reverse path" pair, so the genuinely-different-path projection branch cannot fire on a simple 4-arm crossing built the usual way (each arm typically has one path to each other arm, but you need ≥3 arms for "different path" to mean anything beyond the trivial reverse).
